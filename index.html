Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Arkevo

Straightforward answers regarding our operating methodology, cross-platform media execution, and reporting transparency.

How does Arkevo reduce ACoS without throttling organic sales?
+
We don't simply slash budgets across the board. We systematically isolate "bleeders"—unproductive search terms and broad queries that consume ad dollars without generating conversions—and apply aggressive negative exact targeting. By reallocating that freed capital into high-converting exact match keywords and placement multipliers with high conversion propensity, your organic rank accelerates while your blended TACoS decreases.
Do you manage both Amazon Ads and Walmart Connect simultaneously?
+
Yes. In fact, our dual-platform mastery is one of our greatest competitive advantages. We harvest proven converting search terms and customer intent data from Amazon to rapidly build and scale Walmart Connect campaigns, avoiding the costly trial-and-error period common with agencies that only specialize in a single channel.
What is included in your Catalog Management & SEO service?
+
We audit and optimize product titles, 5 key feature bullets, and backend search terms (staying strictly under character caps to prevent indexing penalties). We also structure variation families, diagnose suppressed or stranded listings, and develop conversion-driven A+ Content (formerly EBC) and Brand Stores designed to elevate your unit session percentage.
How are you different from traditional advertising agencies?
+
Most traditional agencies charge fixed monthly retainers plus a percentage of your ad spend. That model creates perverse incentives: they earn more when you spend more, regardless of whether your bottom-line profit survives. Arkevo operates on a profit-first philosophy with transparent, accountable reporting and access to our live client dashboard so you see every dollar's contribution margin in real-time.
Do you work with both Seller Central (3P) and Vendor Central (1P)?
+
Yes. We manage both 3P Seller Central accounts and 1P Vendor Central accounts. Strategy shifts considerably between the two—specifically around buy-box suppression, chargebacks, Amazon purchase order cadence, and retail margins—and our team executes with tailored architectures for each environment.
How do we access the live analytics reporting dashboard?
+
Every Arkevo partner gets direct access to our unified analytics dashboard (accessible at /dashboard.html), tracking spend, revenue, ACoS trends, keyword-level conversion metrics, and Amazon vs Walmart platform splits updated with granular transparency.
